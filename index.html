Edge Device Hardware Mapping

📷
Lateral Monocular Camera (30 FPS)

Tracks sagittal plane landmarks (Hip, Knee, Ankle, Foot) to measure gross flexion angles and step timing.

⚡
4 Wearable 6-DOF IMUs (100 Hz)

Mounted bilaterally on Left/Right Thigh and Shank to compute relative orientation $Q_{rel} = Q_{thigh}^{-1} \times Q_{shank}$ and angular velocities.

🦶
8 Plantar FSR Insole Sensors (100 Hz)

Bilateral 4-zone force resistive sensors (Heel, 5th Meta, 1st/2nd Meta, Hallux) for relative loading and COP proxy.

🧠
Raspberry Pi / Edge Microprocessor

Executes local Butterworth digital filtering, gait cycle segmentation, feature fusion, and real-time tree inference.

Privacy Guarantee: Pseudonymous session ID only. Biometric telemetry and patient questionnaire data remain isolated on the edge device.
